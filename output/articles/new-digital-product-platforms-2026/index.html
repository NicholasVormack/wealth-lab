Platform Recommendations

Gumroad

Best for: General digital products, easy setup, built-in audience discovery. Fee: 10% + $0.50 per sale on free plan. Use when: You want simplicity and don't mind higher fees for low volume.

Payhip

Best for: Lowest fees at volume, product-focused storefront. Fee: 5% free, drops to 2% on $29/mo Plus plan. Use when: You're selling $500+/month and want to keep more margin.

Ko-fi

Best for: Tips + digital products, audience-first creators. Fee: 5% on sales, or 0% with Gold ($12/mo). Use when: You have an engaged audience that tips, and sell occasional products.

Lemon Squeezy

Best for: Digital products, global tax handling (they handle VAT/sales tax). Fee: 5% + $0.50 per sale. Use when: You sell internationally and want tax compliance handled.

Beacons

Best for: Link-in-bio sellers who want shop + link page combined. Fee: 9% free, 0% on Store Pro ($30/mo). Use when: Your traffic comes from social media bios.

Bottom Line

Start with Gumroad or Ko-fi (free to start). Move to Payhip Plus once you're making $500+/month. Create one digital product, list it, and start promoting. Your first sale validates the model.
