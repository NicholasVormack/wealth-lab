# Sell Digital Products With $0 Startup Cost

Digital products are the highest-margin business you can start with $0. Create once, sell forever. No inventory, no shipping, no manufacturing.

## Why Digital Products Win

| Factor | Digital Products | Physical Products |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Startup cost | $0 | $500–$5,000+ |
| Inventory | None | Required |
| Shipping | None | Required |
| Margins | 95%+ | 30–60% |
| Scaling | Instant | Limited by stock |

## What You Can Sell (All Free to Create)

- **Notion templates** — Build in Notion free tier, export as template link
- **Spreadsheet calculators** — Google Sheets or Excel, share as template
- **Checklists & SOPs** — Google Docs, export as PDF
- **Short ebooks/guides** — Write in Google Docs, export as PDF
- **Calculators & micro-tools** — HTML/JavaScript, host on GitHub Pages
- **Lightroom/photo presets** — Create in free photo editors
- **Social media templates** — Canva free tier

## Step-by-Step Launch

### Day 1: Pick Your Product
Choose one thing you can create in under 4 hours. Examples:
- "Freelance Rate Calculator" spreadsheet
- "Side Hustle Idea List" PDF (50 ideas)
- "Notion Finance Tracker" template
- "Meal Prep on a Budget" guide

### Day 2: Create It
Use only free tools:
- Google Docs/Sheets for documents
- Canva free for graphics
- HTML/JavaScript for calculators

### Day 3: Set Up Storefront
1. Create a free Gumroad or Ko-fi account
2. Upload your product
3. Set your price (even $0 for a lead magnet)
4. Copy your product link

### Day 4–7: Drive Traffic
- Publish an article on Medium/Dev.to about the problem your product solves
- Share your micro-tool on Reddit and X
- Include your product link in your bio and content

## Pricing Strategy

- **Lead magnet ($0)**: Build email list, upsell later
- **Low-ticket ($7–$27)**: Impulse buy, high volume
- **Mid-ticket ($47–$97)**: Serious buyers, fewer sales needed
- **High-ticket ($197+)**: Requires trust, sell after free/low-ticket

Start with $0–$7 to get your first sales and reviews. Raise prices as you get testimonials.

## Bottom Line

Digital products + free storefronts + organic traffic = a $0-capital business that scales while you sleep. Create one product this week. List it free on Gumroad. Start promoting.
