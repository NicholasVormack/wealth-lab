Strategy: Stack Bonuses

  1. Open multiple brokerage accounts simultaneously — each bonus is independent
  2. Start with free stock bonuses (Robinhood, Webull, Moomoo) — no deposit required
  3. Stack with cash bonuses (E*TRADE, J.P. Morgan) — requires deposit
  4. Transfer existing accounts — Interactive Brokers pays up to $1,000 for referrals, 1% match on transfers
  5. Reinvest bonuses — Move free stock cash into index funds (FXAIX, FZROX, VOO)

Realistic earnings from bonuses: $500–$3,000 in first month if you open 5–10 accounts.

Important Rules

  • Read the terms: Bonuses often require holding deposits for 60 days to 1 year
  • One bonus per customer: Most platforms limit to one account per person
  • Tax implications: Bonuses are taxable income
  • Minimum deposits: Some bonuses require $25K–$500K (Schwab referral), others $0–$50

Bottom Line

Brokerage bonuses are free seed capital. Claim them, then invest everything into low-cost index funds. Don't let bonuses sit in cash — put them to work compounding.
