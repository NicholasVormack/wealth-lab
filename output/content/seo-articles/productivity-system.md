# The $0 Productivity System for Online Builders

You don't need Notion, Todoist, or any paid app to be productive. You need a system. Here's one that costs $0.

## The Core Principle

One thing at a time. One day at a time. One week at a time.

## Daily System (30 Minutes Planning + 2 Hours Execution)

### Morning Planning (10 minutes)

Write down:
1. **One main task** — the most important thing today
2. **Three small tasks** — quick wins that support the main task
3. **One distribution action** — post on social, reply to comments, send an email

### Focus Block (90 minutes)

Work on your main task with no distractions:
- Phone in another room
- Browser closed except for what you need
- Timer set for 90 minutes
- No email, no social, no "research"

### Distribution Block (30 minutes)

- Post today's content on one platform
- Reply to all comments
- Engage with 5 other accounts in your niche

### Evening Review (10 minutes)

- Did you complete the main task?
- What blocked you?
- What's the main task for tomorrow?

## Weekly System (Sunday Planning)

1. **Review last week** — what worked, what didn't
2. **Set next week's main goal** — one measurable outcome
3. **Plan 3–5 content pieces** — articles, tools, social posts
4. **Schedule distribution** — which platforms, which days

## Monthly System (First Day of Month)

1. **Review metrics** — visitors, revenue, email subscribers
2. **Identify the winner** — what's driving results?
3. **Double down** — plan more content around the winner
4. **Set next month's revenue goal** — realistic, specific

## The "No Zero Days" Rule

Every day, do at least one thing that moves your business forward:
- Write one paragraph of an article
- Post one tweet
- Reply to one comment
- Fix one bug on your site
- Research one keyword

One small action beats zero big actions.

## Batch Working

Group similar tasks together:

- **Monday**: Write all articles for the week
- **Tuesday**: Create all social posts for the week
- **Wednesday**: Build/update micro-tools
- **Thursday**: Distribute content, engage with audience
- **Friday**: Review metrics, plan next week

This prevents context switching and saves hours.

## Bottom Line

Productivity isn't about apps. It's about showing up daily, doing the main task, and distributing your work. Use free tools, batch your work, and never have a zero day.
