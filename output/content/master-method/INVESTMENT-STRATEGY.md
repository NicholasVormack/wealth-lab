## PHASE 3: Dollar-Cost Averaging (The Automation Engine)

**DCA = Invest the same dollar amount at regular intervals, regardless of price.**

| Month | Amount Invested | Share Price | Shares Bought |
|-------|----------------|-------------|---------------|
| Jan | $100 | $10 | 10.00 |
| Feb | $100 | $8 | 12.50 |
| Mar | $100 | $12 | 8.33 |
| **Total** | **$300** | **Avg: $7.94** | **30.83** |

**You bought more shares when prices were low, fewer when high.** Your average cost is lower than the average price.

**Setup:**
1. Decide your monthly investment amount (start with $25, $50, or $100 — whatever side hustle income produces)
2. Set up automatic transfer from checking to brokerage
3. Set up automatic purchase of your chosen index fund
4. Enable **DRIP** (Dividend Reinvestment Program) — dividends automatically buy more shares
5. Check quarterly, not daily

**Math:** $200/month at 7% average annual return = ~$250,000 in 30 years. Only $72,000 of that is your money. The rest is compound growth.
