# Outsourcing Your $0-Cost Business — When and How

You can't do everything forever. Here is when and how to outsource as your revenue grows, without spending money you don't have.

## When NOT to Outsource

Don't outsource until you meet ALL of these:
- ✅ You're consistently making $500+/month
- ✅ You've validated that your offer converts
- ✅ You're spending 20+ hours/week on repetitive tasks
- ✅ You have a clear process that someone else can follow

If you haven't hit these milestones, keep doing it yourself. Outsourcing too early kills cash flow.

## The Outsourcing Hierarchy

Start at the bottom and work up as revenue grows:

### Level 1: Free Automation (Start Here)

Before hiring anyone, automate:

| Task | Free Automation |
|------|----------------|
| Social posting | Buffer free tier, native scheduling |
| Email sequences | Mailchimp free tier (automation) |
| File backups | Google Drive auto-sync |
| Link tracking | Bitly free, UTM parameters |

### Level 2: Task-Based Help ($5–$25 per task)

Once you have $500+/month, hire for single tasks:

- **Thumbnail design** — Fiverr ($5–$15 per thumbnail)
- **Video editing** — Fiverr/Upwork ($20–$50 per video)
- **Transcription** — Otter.ai free tier, or Rev ($1.25/min)
- **Keyword research** — Virtual assistant on Fiverr ($10–$20 per batch)

### Level 3: Part-Time VA ($300–$800/month)

Once you're making $1K+/month consistently:

- **Virtual assistant** — Philippines-based VAs on OnlineJobs.ph ($400–$800/month)
- **Tasks they can handle**: Email inbox, social engagement, comment moderation, data entry, basic research

### Level 4: Specialized Help ($1,000+/month)

Once you're making $3K+/month:

- **Content writer** — One article per week ($100–$300/article)
- **Video editor** — One video per week ($200–$500/video)
- **Community manager** — Discord/Telegram moderation ($500–$1,000/month)

## What to Outsource First

Start with the task you hate most that's also easy to teach:

1. **Video editing** — Record yourself, send raw footage to an editor
2. **Thumbnail design** — Give them the template, they make variations
3. **Social posting** — Give them the content, they schedule it
4. **Email management** — Give them templates, they handle replies

Never outsource strategy, product creation, or customer relationships. Those stay with you.

| Twitter/X | Direct DMs to creators | Free |

## The "Hire One Person" Rule

Don't hire a team. Hire ONE person who can do ONE thing well. Train them. Let them prove themselves. Then add more work or hire a second person.

## Reinvestment Math

| Monthly Revenue | Reinvest in Outsourcing |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| $500 | $0 — keep doing it yourself |
| $1,000 | $100–$200/month (1 VA task per week) |
| $2,000 | $300–$500/month (1 VA + occasional video editing) |
| $5,000 | $1,000–$1,500/month (part-time VA + writer) |

## Bottom Line

Outsource when you're making money, not before. Start with one task, one person, one tool. Reinvest a small percentage of your profits. Build a team slowly, one hire at a time.
