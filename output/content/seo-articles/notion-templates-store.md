# Sell Notion Templates With $0 Startup Cost

Notion templates are one of the easiest digital products to create and sell. You need a free Notion account, a template worth paying for, and a free storefront like Gumroad or Ko-fi.

## Why Notion Templates Sell

- **Low friction**: Buyers duplicate the template into their own Notion workspace with one click
- **No delivery hassle**: Notion handles the duplication, you just share a link
- **High perceived value**: A well-organized template saves buyers hours of setup time
- **Evergreen**: Good templates sell for months or years without updates

## How to Build a Template (Free)

1. **Create a free Notion account** at notion.so
2. **Build your template** using Notion's blocks: databases, pages, toggles, properties
3. **Make it visually appealing**: Use icons, cover images, clear headings
4. **Add instructions**: Include a "How to use this template" page
5. **Test it**: Duplicate it yourself to make sure everything works

## Sharing Your Template

In Notion:
1. Click "Share" in the top right
2. Toggle "Share to web" on
3. Toggle "Allow duplicate as template" on
4. Copy the public link

This link is what buyers click to duplicate your template into their workspace.

## Setting Up Your Storefront (Free)

### Gumroad (Recommended)
1. Create a free Gumroad account
2. Click "Add product" → "Membership" or "Digital product"
3. Paste your Notion template link as the delivery method
4. Set your price (even $0 for a lead magnet)
5. Publish and copy your product URL

### Ko-fi (Alternative)
1. Create a free Ko-fi account
2. Add a "Shop" item
3. Upload your Notion template link
4. Set price and publish
