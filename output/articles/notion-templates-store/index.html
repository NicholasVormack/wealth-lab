Sell Notion Templates With $0 Startup Cost

2026-11-13 · notion-templates, digital-products, zero-dollar, make-money-online

Notion templates are one of the easiest digital products to create and sell. You need a free Notion account, a template worth paying for, and a free storefront like Gumroad or Ko-fi.

Why Notion Templates Sell

  • Low friction: Buyers duplicate the template into their own Notion workspace with one click
  • No delivery hassle: Notion handles the duplication, you just share a link
  • High perceived value: A well-organized template saves buyers hours of setup time
  • Evergreen: Good templates sell for months or years without updates

What Templates to Build

| Template Type | Who Buys It | Price Range | |--------------|-------------|-------------| | Finance tracker | Freelancers, side hustlers | $7–$15 | | Content planner | Bloggers, YouTubers, creators | $12–$25 | | Student OS | College students | $5–$15 | | Freelance CRM | Freelancers, consultants | $15–$30 | | Habit tracker | Productivity enthusiasts | $5–$10 | | Side hustle planner | Aspiring entrepreneurs | $10–$20 |

How to Build a Template (Free)

  1. Create a free Notion account at notion.so
  2. Build your template using Notion's blocks: databases, pages, toggles, properties
  3. Make it visually appealing: Use icons, cover images, clear headings
  4. Add instructions: Include a "How to use this template" page
  5. Test it: Duplicate it yourself to make sure everything works

Sharing Your Template

In Notion:

  1. Click "Share" in the top right
  2. Toggle "Share to web" on
  3. Toggle "Allow duplicate as template" on
  4. Copy the public link

This link is what buyers click to duplicate your template into their workspace.

Setting Up Your Storefront (Free)

Gumroad (Recommended)

  1. Create a free Gumroad account
  2. Click "Add product" → "Membership" or "Digital product"
  3. Paste your Notion template link as the delivery method
  4. Set your price (even $0 for a lead magnet)
  5. Publish and copy your product URL

Ko-fi (Alternative)

  1. Create a free Ko-fi account
  2. Add a "Shop" item
  3. Upload your Notion template link
  4. Set price and publish

Promoting Your Template

Where to Share

  • Reddit: r/Notion, r/Productivity, r/sidehustle (read rules first)
  • Twitter/X: Share screenshots of your template in use
  • LinkedIn: Post about the problem your template solves
  • TikTok: Screen-record yourself using the template (satisfying "setup" videos perform well)
  • Pinterest: Create pin images showing template pages

Content That Sells

  • "How I organize my finances with Notion (free template)"
  • "My content planning system that saved me 5 hours/week"
  • "Student Notion setup for straight A's"
  • "Freelance client tracker that actually works"

Pricing Strategy

  • Free lead magnet: Build email list, collect testimonials
  • $5–$10: Impulse buy, high volume
  • $15–$25: Serious buyers, fewer sales needed
  • $27+: Premium templates with video tutorials

Start with a free template to get reviews, then launch paid versions.

Realistic Numbers

  • Template creation time: 2–10 hours (one-time)
  • Sales per month (early): 0–10 sales
  • Sales per month (established): 50–200+ sales
  • Revenue per month (established): $500–$2,000+

Bottom Line

Notion templates are a $0-capital digital product with recurring sales potential. Build one template this week, list it free on Gumroad, and start promoting. Your first sale validates the model.
