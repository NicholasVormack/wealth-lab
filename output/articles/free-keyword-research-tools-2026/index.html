Free Keyword Research Tools for $0 SEO (2026)

2026-11-13 · keyword-research, free-tools, seo, zero-dollar

You do not need Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Ubersuggest paid plans to find profitable keywords. These free tools are enough to build a programmatic SEO micro-tool empire.

1. Google Keyword Planner (Free with Google Ads account)

You do not need to run ads. Create a Google Ads account, switch to "Expert Mode," and create a dummy campaign without entering payment details. Then access Keyword Planner under Tools & Settings.

Best for: Search volume estimates, competition levels, keyword ideas.

Limit: You get broad ranges (e.g., "100–1K") unless you spend money, but the relative data is still useful.

2. Google Trends (Free)

Compare search interest over time and across regions. Find rising queries before they peak.

Best for: Seasonal trends, topic validation, related queries, geographic interest.

Tip: Use the "Related queries" section to find breakout keywords (+100%+ growth).

3. Google Autosuggest (Free)

Type your seed keyword into Google and let autocomplete suggest completions. These are real searches people are making right now.

Best for: Long-tail question keywords, content ideas.

Tip: Use an underscore _ as a wildcard: best _ for freelancers reveals variations.

4. AnswerThePublic (Free tier)

Visualizes questions people ask around a keyword (who, what, where, when, why, how).

Best for: FAQ content, article outlines, tool feature ideas.

Limit: 3 free searches per day.

5. Keyword Surfer (Free Chrome Extension)

Shows search volume and related keywords directly in Google search results.

Best for: Quick validation while browsing.

6. People Also Ask (Free)

Scroll through Google search results and expand "People Also Ask" boxes. Each question is a potential article or tool page.

Best for: Question-based long-tail keywords, featured snippet targets.

Programmatic Keyword Strategy for Micro-Tools

Take one seed keyword (e.g., "rate calculator") and cross it with modifiers:

| Seed | Modifier | Resulting Page | |------|----------|----------------| | rate calculator | freelance writer | /freelance-writer-rate-calculator/ | | rate calculator | graphic designer | /graphic-designer-rate-calculator/ | | rate calculator | consultant | /consultant-rate-calculator/ | | rate calculator | virtual assistant | /virtual-assistant-rate-calculator/ |

Each page targets a different long-tail keyword. The tool logic is identical; only the examples and copy change.

Bottom Line

Start with Google Autosuggest and People Also Ask. Validate with Keyword Planner. Build one tool page per long-tail keyword. Publish 3–5 pages per week. The compound effect kicks in around month 3.
