Tax Basics for Freelancers and Side Hustlers (2026)

2026-11-14 · taxes, freelancers, side-hustle, self-employment

You don't need a CPA to start. Here's what you need to know about taxes when you're making money online with $0 upfront.

You're a Business (Even If You Don't Feel Like One)

The moment you earn $400+ from self-employment (freelancing, affiliate commissions, digital products, etc.), the IRS considers you self-employed. You need to:

  1. Report all income
  2. Pay self-employment tax
  3. File quarterly estimated taxes (if you expect to owe $1,000+)

Self-Employment Tax

Self-employment tax is 15.3% (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare) on your net earnings. This is IN ADDITION to income tax.

Example: If you make $10,000 net profit:

  • Self-employment tax: ~$1,530
  • Income tax: varies by bracket
  • Total tax burden: ~25–35% of profit

Quarterly Estimated Taxes

If you expect to owe $1,000+ in taxes for the year, you need to pay quarterly:

| Due Date | Period Covered | |----------|---------------| | April 15 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | | June 15 | Apr 1 – May 31 | | Sept 15 | Jun 1 – Aug 31 | | Jan 15 | Sept 1 – Dec 31 |

Use IRS Form 1040-ES to calculate and pay. Free to file through IRS Direct Pay.

Deductible Expenses (Reduce Your Tax)

You can deduct business expenses from your income:

  • Home office (if you have a dedicated space)
  • Internet and phone (business portion)
  • Software and tools (Canva, CapChat, email marketing)
  • Education (courses, books related to your business)
  • Equipment (computer, phone, camera)
  • Professional services (CPA, lawyer)

Keep receipts. Take photos. Store them digitally (Google Drive free tier).

Record Keeping (Free)

You don't need QuickBooks. Use:

  • Google Sheets — Track income and expenses
  • Google Drive — Store receipts and invoices
  • Calendar — Mark tax deadlines

Simple spreadsheet columns: | Date | Source | Amount | Expense Category | Notes | |------|--------|--------|------------------|-------|

Affiliate Income Is Taxable

Affiliate commissions (Systeme.io, ClickBank, Amazon Associates, etc.) are taxable income. You'll receive a 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ from a single platform.

Digital Product Sales Tax

Selling digital products on Gumroad or Ko-fi? They handle sales tax collection in many jurisdictions. But you still report the income on your tax return.

Get a CPA When...

  • You're making $3K+/month consistently
  • You have complex deductions
  • You're incorporating (LLC, S-Corp)
  • You're unsure about quarterly payments

Until then, the IRS website (irs.gov) and free resources are enough.

Bottom Line

Set aside 25–30% of every dollar you earn for taxes. Track income and expenses in a free spreadsheet. File quarterly if you owe $1,000+. Don't let tax fear stop you from starting — but don't ignore it either.
