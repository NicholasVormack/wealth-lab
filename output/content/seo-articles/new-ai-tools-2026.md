## The AI Acceleration Pipeline

Use this workflow to compress content creation from 8 hours to 1.5 hours per article:

1. **Idea Generation** — AI suggests 20 long-tail keywords. You pick the top 5.
2. **Outline Creation** — AI creates H1/H2/H3 structure. You add personal examples.
3. **First Draft** — AI writes 1,500 words. You rewrite intro/conclusion, add affiliate links.
4. **SEO Optimization** — AI suggests title, meta description, keywords. You apply them.
5. **Social Distribution** — AI turns article into X thread, LinkedIn post, TikTok script. You record and post.
6. **Email Sequence** — AI writes 5-email welcome series. You personalize and load into Mailchimp.

**Time saved:** ~10 hours per week per article.
