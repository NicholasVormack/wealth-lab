# New Social Media Platforms That Pay Creators 2026

You don't need 10,000 followers to get paid. These platforms pay based on views, engagement, or direct sales — not follower count.

| Platform | Monetization | Follower Minimum | Best For |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| TikTok Shop | Affiliate + shop commissions | 0 (link to seller account) | Short-form product videos |
| Snapchat Spotlight | Rewards based on views | 0 | Casual, funny, trend-based content |
| Threads | Invite-only Bonus Program (views-based) | 0 | Text posts, casual content |

## TikTok Shop (Fastest to $0 Followers)

TikTok Shop lets you earn commissions from day one if you understand how to navigate the ecosystem. You don't need a large following — you need good product videos.

**How it works:**
1. Apply to TikTok Shop Affiliate
2. Find products in the marketplace
3. Create short videos demonstrating the product
4. Earn commission on every sale through your link

**Best niches:** Beauty, tech gadgets, kitchen gadgets, fitness gear, pet products.

## Substack (Paid Newsletters)

Substack evolved from a newsletter tool into a full social network. You can offer paid subscriptions, accept tips, and create premium content — even with a small following.

**Strategy:**
1. Write one article per week about your niche
2. Offer free posts to build trust
3. Put your best content behind a paywall ($5–$15/month)
4. Use Substack Notes for short-form engagement

**Best niches:** Personal finance, investing, productivity, niche expertise, commentary.

## Snapchat Spotlight

Snapchat Spotlight pays creators based on views, not subscribers. The audience skews under 25.

**Strategy:**
1. Post casual, funny, or trend-based videos
2. Don't need to be on camera in a polished way
3. Payouts based on views
4. Cash out daily at $100 minimum

## Threads (Meta)

Meta has been testing an invite-only Bonus Program on Threads that rewards creators for posting original, high-quality content. Payouts depend on engagement milestones — usually based on views.

**Strategy:**
1. Post original text-based content daily
2. Engage with replies
3. Build engagement milestones
4. Wait for invite to Bonus Program

## Pinterest (Search Engine, Not Social Network)

People go to Pinterest to plan, buy, and learn. Perfect for promoting micro-tools and affiliate links.

**Strategy:**
1. Create vertical pins (1000 × 1500 pixels)
2. Use bold text overlay
3. Link to your micro-tools on GitHub Pages
4. Pin 5–10 times per day
5. Organize into niche-specific boards

## Bottom Line

Don't wait for followers. Start earning on day one with TikTok Shop affiliate, Substack paid newsletters, Snapchat Spotlight, and Pinterest affiliate links. Pick one platform, post daily, scale what works.
