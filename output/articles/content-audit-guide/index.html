Content Audit — Find What's Working (And What's Not)

2026-11-14 · content-audit, seo-audit, analytics, optimization

Most creators publish and forget. Smart creators audit and optimize. Here's how to do a content audit with free tools.

Why Audit?

You can't improve what you don't measure. An audit tells you:

  • Which articles get traffic (and which don't)
  • Which micro-tools get used (and which don't)
  • Which affiliate links convert (and which don't)
  • Where to focus your limited time

Step 1: Gather Your Data (Free)

Google Search Console (Free)

  1. Verify your site (HTML file or DNS)
  2. Go to Performance → Search results
  3. Export the last 90 days of data

Look for:

  • Pages with impressions but low clicks (title/meta description needs work)
  • Pages with high clicks but low conversions (landing page needs work)
  • Keywords you rank for but don't target (content opportunity)

Your Micro-Tools

Check each tool's usage:

  • How many people visit the page?
  • How many use the tool (click the calculate button)?
  • How many click through to affiliate links?

Use the Wealth Lab analytics tracker (output/analytics-tracker.js) to collect this data.

Your Articles (Medium/Dev.to)

Check:

  • Views per article
  • Read time (are people finishing?)
  • Comments and engagement
  • External clicks (affiliate link clicks)

Step 2: Score Each Piece of Content

Rate each article and tool 1–5 on these criteria:

| Criterion | What It Means | |-----------|---------------| | Traffic | How many people visit? | | Engagement | Do they read/use it? | | Conversion | Do they click affiliate links? | | Revenue | Does it generate income? | | Maintenance | How much work to keep it current? |

Score 20–25: Star content. Double down. Score 10–19: Good. Optimize and promote more. Score 0–9: Poor. Improve, repurpose, or retire.

Step 3: Act on the Results

Double Down on Winners

If an article gets traffic but no affiliate clicks:

  • Add more affiliate links
  • Write a stronger call-to-action
  • Create a dedicated micro-tool for that topic

If a tool gets usage but no revenue:

  • Add affiliate links to related products
  • Create a paid version with more features
  • Add email capture to build your list

Fix Underperformers

If an article has impressions but low clicks:

  • Rewrite the title (more compelling)
  • Improve the meta description
  • Add a featured image

If a tool has traffic but no usage:

  • Simplify the interface
  • Add clearer instructions
  • Reduce the number of input fields

Retire Dead Content

If an article gets zero traffic after 3 months of promotion:

  • Don't delete it (it might rank eventually)
  • But stop promoting it
  • Repurpose it into a different format (video, social thread)

Step 4: Content Gap Analysis

Look at what your competitors rank for that you don't:

  1. Find a competitor's top pages
  2. Check what keywords they rank for
  3. Identify keywords you don't target
  4. Build micro-tools or articles for those keywords

This is your fastest path to new traffic.

Monthly Audit Routine

| Week | Task | Time | |------|------|------| | Week 1 | Check Google Search Console | 30 min | | Week 2 | Review micro-tool usage data | 30 min | | Week 3 | Analyze article performance (Medium/Dev.to) | 30 min | | Week 4 | Plan next month based on findings | 60 min |

Total: 2.5 hours per month

Bottom Line

Publish less, audit more. One well-optimized article outperforms ten neglected ones. Run your audit monthly, double down on winners, and cut what doesn't work.
