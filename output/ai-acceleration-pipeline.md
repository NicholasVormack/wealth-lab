# AI Acceleration Pipeline

**Principle:** AI compresses execution. Human judgment adds value. Never publish AI-only content.

**Total time saved:** ~10 hours per week per article

**Key rule:** AI writes the draft. Human adds the soul. Publish only when it sounds like you.

---

## Step 1: Idea Generation

**AI Prompt:**
```
Give me 20 long-tail keyword ideas for [NICHE]. Each must be a specific question someone would type into Google. Focus on buyer intent and problem-aware searches.
```

**Human Action:** Pick the top 5 ideas. Add your own perspective or experience to each.

**Time Saved:** 30 min → 2 min

---

## Step 2: Outline Creation

**AI Prompt:**
```
Create a detailed outline for an article about [TOPIC]. Include H1, H2, H3 sections. Add FAQ section at end. Target word count 1,500-2,000. Make it practical and actionable, not generic.
```

**Human Action:** Reorder sections. Add personal examples. Remove anything generic.

**Time Saved:** 45 min → 5 min

---

## Step 3: First Draft

**AI Prompt:**
```
Write a 1,500-word article based on this outline: [OUTLINE]. Write in a helpful, conversational tone. Use short paragraphs. Include specific examples. Avoid generic AI phrases like 'in today's digital landscape' or 'it's important to note'.
```

**Human Action:** Rewrite the intro and conclusion. Add your personal story. Insert your micro-tool links and affiliate links naturally.

**Time Saved:** 3 hours → 45 min

---

## Step 4: SEO Optimization

**AI Prompt:**
```
Review this article for SEO: [ARTICLE]. Suggest: 1) Better title (under 60 chars), 2) Meta description (under 160 chars), 3) 5 relevant keywords to include naturally, 4) Internal linking opportunities.
```

**Human Action:** Apply changes. Add your meta tags. Link to your other articles.

**Time Saved:** 45 min → 10 min

---

## Step 5: Social Distribution

**AI Prompt:**
```
Turn this article into: 1) X thread (5 tweets), 2) LinkedIn post, 3) Pinterest pin description, 4) TikTok script (60 seconds). Keep each platform's style in mind.
```

**Human Action:** Record the TikTok/Reel. Post manually. Engage with comments.

**Time Saved:** 2 hours → 20 min

---

## Step 6: Email Sequence

**AI Prompt:**
```
Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers interested in [NICHE]. Each email under 300 words. Include one soft pitch in email 4. End each with a question to encourage replies.
```

**Human Action:** Personalize with your voice. Add your actual product/affiliate links. Load into Mailchimp/MailerLite.

**Time Saved:** 2 hours → 20 min

---

## Daily Workflow (Compressed)

| Task | Without AI | With AI Pipeline |
|------|------------|------------------|
| Idea + Outline | 75 min | 7 min |
| Write Draft | 3 hours | 45 min |
| SEO + Optimize | 45 min | 10 min |
| Social Distribution | 2 hours | 20 min |
| Email Sequence | 2 hours | 20 min |
| **Total per article** | **~8.5 hours** | **~1.5 hours** |

**You can now publish 3-5x more content with the same time investment.**
