# Best Free Email Marketing Platforms 2026

Mailchimp's free tier caps you at 250 contacts and 500 emails. There are much better free options. Here are the best free email platforms for 2026.

## Free Tier Comparison

| Platform | Free Subscribers | Free Emails/Month | Automation | Best For |
|----------|-----------------|------------------|------------|----------|
| Kit (formerly ConvertKit) | Up to 10,000 | Unlimited | Basic (1 sequence) | Creators, digital product sellers |
| Brevo | Unlimited contacts | 9,000 | Full | High volume, low frequency |
| MailerLite | 500 | 12,000 | Basic | Small businesses, beginners |
| Beehiiv | 2,500 | Unlimited | Basic | Newsletter creators |
| Substack | Unlimited | Unlimited | Basic | Paid newsletters (takes 10% commission) |
| Mailchimp | 250 | 500 | Basic | Broad small business toolkit |

## Why Kit (ConvertKit) Is the Best Free Option

Kit's free plan supports up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited email sends. That's 40x more subscribers than Mailchimp's free plan. For a creator just starting out, Kit means you won't hit a paywall until you have a real audience.

**Kit free tier includes:**
- Up to 10,000 subscribers
- Unlimited email sends
- Visual automation builder (limited to 1 sequence on free)
- Tag-based segmentation
- Built-in commerce tools for selling digital products
- Newsletter referral system

## Brevo (Best for Volume)

Brevo gives you unlimited contacts and 9,000 emails per month on the free tier, with full automation. If you need to send a lot of emails to a large list, Brevo is the best free option.

**Brevo free tier includes:**
- Unlimited contacts
- 9,000 emails/month (~300/day)
- Full automation
- Email templates
- CRM features

## MailerLite (Best for Beginners)

MailerLite's free plan includes 500 subscribers and 12,000 emails per month, with basic email automation. Good for small businesses just starting out.

## Substack (Best for Paid Newsletters)

Substack lets you offer paid subscriptions, accept tips, and create premium content. They take 10% commission but handle everything — payments, deliverability, list management.

**Strategy:** Start with Kit free tier. Move to Brevo if you need more automation. Use Substack if you want paid subscriptions from day one.

## Email Setup Checklist

```
□ Create Kit (ConvertKit) free account
□ Set up audience/list
□ Create signup form (embed or hosted)
□ Create lead magnet (free PDF, checklist, or tool)
□ Set up 5-email welcome sequence
□ Place signup form on micro-tool pages
□ Add link in bio to signup page
□ Send 1 email per week
```

## Bottom Line

Don't start with Mailchimp's 250-contact limit. Start with Kit's 10,000-subscriber free tier. Build your list from day one. Send one email per week. Your list is the only audience you truly own.
