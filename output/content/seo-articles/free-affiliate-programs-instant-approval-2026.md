# 7 Affiliate Programs With Instant Approval (No Website Required)

You can start affiliate marketing today with $0 and no website. These programs approve you instantly and let you start promoting immediately.

## 1. Systeme.io
- **Commission**: 40% recurring (lifetime)
- **Approval**: Instant
- **Website required**: No
- **Best for**: Promoting an all-in-one business platform

## 2. ClickBank
- **Commission**: Up to 90% on some digital products
- **Approval**: Instant (most offers)
- **Website required**: No
- **Best for**: Digital products in health, wealth, dating niches

## 3. Digistore24
- **Commission**: Varies by product (often 40-50%)
- **Approval**: Fast/Instant
- **Website required**: No
- **Best for**: Digital courses and software

## 4. ShareASale
- **Commission**: Varies by merchant (5-50%)
- **Approval**: Fast (1-2 days typically)
- **Website required**: Recommended but lenient
- **Best for**: Wide variety of physical and digital products

## 5. Amazon Associates
- **Commission**: 1-10% by category
- **Approval**: Requires application + 3 qualified sales within 180 days
- **Website required**: Yes (social media profiles sometimes accepted)
- **Best for**: Physical products, high trust brand

## 6. Fiverr Affiliates
- **Commission**: $15-$150 per first-time buyer
- **Approval**: Fast
- **Website required**: No
- **Best for**: Freelance services

## 7. Gumroad
- **Commission**: Varies by creator
- **Approval**: Instant (sign up as creator/affiliate)
- **Website required**: No
- **Best for**: Digital products, templates, ebooks

## How to Promote Without a Website

1. **Medium**: Write articles, include affiliate links in your bio and content
2. **Dev.to**: Publish tech/business tutorials with relevant tool links
3. **Reddit**: Answer questions in relevant subreddits (read rules first, don't spam)
4. **YouTube**: Review tools, put links in description
5. **TikTok/Instagram**: Short-form content with link in bio (Linktree free)

## The Strategy

Pick one program. Create 10 pieces of content around it. Distribute daily. Track what converts. Scale what works.
