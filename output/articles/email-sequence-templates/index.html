Email Sequence Templates — Free 5-Email Welcome Series

2026-11-18 · email-marketing, templates, welcome-sequence, zero-dollar

Five emails, sent over the first week. The structure is what matters: deliver, prove, story, offer, conversation.

The curly-brace tokens below are merge tags. Every email platform supports them, and most will autocomplete them once you type the opening braces. Replace the words in them with your own details before sending.

Email 1 — Deliver the lead magnet (send immediately)

Subject: Your {{lead_magnet_name}} is here

Hi {{first_name}},

Here is the {{lead_magnet_name}} you asked for: {{download_link}}

I built it because {{the_specific_problem_it_solves}}. It took me longer than I expected to get right, so you do not have to repeat that.

Over the next few days I will send a few more free tools that solve adjacent problems. Nothing to buy — just useful things.

— {{your_name}}

P.S. Reply and tell me your biggest {{topic}} problem right now. I read every reply.

Email 2 — Point at your best free tool (day 1)

Subject: The tool I use most

Hi {{first_name}},

Short one today. This is the tool I keep coming back to: {{tool_name}}.

It runs in the browser, takes about thirty seconds, and answers {{the_specific_question}}. No account needed.

Try it: {{tool_link}}

If it saves you time, tell me what you used it for. I build the next one from those replies.

Email 3 — A specific story (day 3)

Subject: What my first month actually looked like

Hi {{first_name}},

Being honest about the boring part: my first month produced almost nothing. I built things nobody asked for and wondered why nobody came.

What changed was scope. Instead of one big thing, I shipped one small tool, one article, and one post per day for a month. That produced {{the_measurable_result}} — small, but it proved the loop worked.

The pattern, if you want to copy it:

1. One narrow tool that answers a single question

2. One article that explains the question properly

3. One post per day pointing at the tool

That is it. No launch, no audience, no budget.

Email 4 — One honest offer (day 5)

Subject: The paid version, if you want it

Hi {{first_name}},

The free tools stay free. This email is only for anyone who wants the next step.

{{product_name}} is a {{product_type}} that {{specific_outcome_it_produces}}. I built it after {{the_reason}} because doing it by hand every time was wasting hours.

Price: {{price}}. Link: {{product_link}}

If you are not sure, reply and ask. I would rather answer a question than have you buy something you do not need.

— {{your_name}}

Email 5 — Ask a question (day 7)

Subject: Quick question

Hi {{first_name}},

It has been a week. What is working for you right now, and what is not?

Just reply to this email. No pitch, no form. The answers decide what I build next, so they genuinely shape the product.

Before you send

  1. Replace every merge tag with real content — a live email containing {{product_name}} is worse than no email at all
  2. Send yourself the whole sequence and click every link on a phone
  3. Set the delays to whatever your platform supports: immediate, then days 1, 3, 5, 7
  4. Keep the plain text — heavy HTML templates land in spam more often
