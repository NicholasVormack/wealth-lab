# SPEED-TO-CASH CHECKLIST — 30-Day Sprint to First $1,000

**Goal:** Compress time-to-first-$1,000 from 90 days to 30 days.
**Strategy:** Stack UGC + Services + Recurring Affiliates simultaneously. Do not wait.

---

## WEEK 1: Foundation & First Cash (Days 1–7)

### Day 1 — Niche & Micro-Tool
- [ ] Pick ONE micro-niche (personal finance, fitness, side hustles, etc.)
- [ ] Write 20 content ideas on that niche
- [ ] Deploy one micro-tool to GitHub Pages (use existing templates in output/)
- [ ] Add email capture form to landing page

### Day 2 — UGC Portfolio (Zero Followers)
- [ ] Create 3 sample UGC videos (phone + CapCut, free)
- [ ] Topics: "3 free tools for [niche]", "How I use [calculator]", "Stop overpaying for [competitor]"
- [ ] Sign up for Influee, Promote, or TikTok Creative Center (all free)
- [ ] Upload 3 portfolio videos

### Day 3 — Service Offer
- [ ] Create productized service: "I'll build your [calculator/tracker/site]" — $50–$150
- [ ] Post offer on Reddit (r/forhire, r/slavelabour), LinkedIn, X
- [ ] Send 5 direct messages to potential clients

### Day 4 — Affiliate Foundation
- [ ] Join Systeme.io (40% recurring, instant approval)
- [ ] Join ClickBank (instant approval, digital products)
- [ ] Join ShareASale (fast approval)
- [ ] Place affiliate links in micro-tool descriptions and articles

### Day 5 — Email Capture
- [ ] Create lead magnet: "7 Free Calculators" checklist or similar
- [ ] Set up Mailchimp/MailerLite free tier (2,000 contacts)
- [ ] Configure the site-wide email capture form (capture.config.json)
- [ ] Load 5-email welcome sequence (already in output/email-sequences/)

### Day 6 — Launch Content
- [ ] Publish 1 SEO article (Medium + Dev.to + your site)
- [ ] Film 1 UGC video, post to TikTok/Reels/Shorts
- [ ] Link in bio: micro-tool + affiliate offer

### Day 7 — First Outreach
- [ ] Comment on 10 posts in your niche
- [ ] Send 5 LinkedIn connection requests
- [ ] Reply to all comments on your content
- [ ] Review what got traction. Double down.

**Week 1 Target:** First $0–$100 (UGC gig or service client)

## IF YOU'RE STUCK AT DAY 14

**Problem:** No traffic to micro-tools.
**Fix:** Post more UGC videos. Comment on more niche posts. Share in relevant subreddits.

**Problem:** No service inquiries.
**Fix:** Lower price by 30%. Rewrite offer to be more specific. Post in more places.

**Problem:** No affiliate clicks.
**Fix:** Place links higher in articles. Write honest review content. Add CTA buttons.

**Problem:** No email subscribers.
**Fix:** Make lead magnet more specific. Place signup form above the fold. Offer immediate value.
