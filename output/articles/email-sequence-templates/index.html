Email Sequence Templates — Free Welcome Series

2026-11-14 · email-marketing, templates, welcome-sequence, zero-dollar

Copy and paste these 5-email welcome sequences. Customize the bracketed text and start sending today.

Email 1: Deliver the Lead Magnet (Send Immediately)

Subject: Your [lead magnet name] is here

Hi [First Name],

Here’s the [lead magnet] you requested: [link to download/page].

I built this because I was tired of [problem the lead magnet solves]. I spent [time period] figuring it out so you don’t have to.

Over the next few days, I’ll share more free tools and resources that helped me [achieve result]. Keep an eye on your inbox.

Best, [Your Name]

P.S. Reply to this email and tell me: what’s your biggest [niche] challenge right now? I read every reply.

Email 2: Share Your Best Free Tool (Day 1)

Subject: The free tool I use every day

Hi [First Name],

I want to share something that saved me hours last week: [name of your micro-tool].

It’s free, takes 30 seconds to use, and helps you [specific benefit].

Try it here: [your micro-tool URL]

No signup. No email required. Just use it and let me know what you think.

Best, [Your Name]

Email 3: Personal Story (Day 3)

Subject: How I made my first $100 online

Hi [First Name],

I want to be honest with you.

When I started, I made $0 for the first [time period]. I almost quit.

What changed? I stopped looking for shortcuts and started building one small thing at a time:

  1. One micro-tool
  2. One article per week
  3. One social post per day

Within [time period], I made my first $100. Not life-changing money, but proof that the model works.

The tools I used are all free. I’ve linked them below:

  • [Tool 1]
  • [Tool 2]
  • [Tool 3]

You can do the same. Start with one thing.

Best, [Your Name]

Email 4: Soft Pitch (Day 5)

Subject: Something I built for you

Hi [First Name],

After sending you free tools for a few days, I want to tell you about [product name].

It’s a [product type] that helps you [specific benefit]. I built it because [reason].

Normally [competitor price], but for my email list it’s [your price].

Get it here: [Gumroad/Ko-fi link]

No pressure — the free tools are still free. This is just for anyone who wants the next level.

Best, [Your Name]

Email 5: Ask a Question (Day 7)

Subject: Quick question

Hi [First Name],

It’s been a week since you joined. I’d love to know: what’s working for you, and what’s not?

Reply to this email. I read every single response and use them to decide what to build next.

No sales pitch this time. Just curious.

Best, [Your Name]

How to Use These

  1. Copy each email into your email platform (Mailchimp, MailerLite, ConvertKit free tier)
  2. Replace [bracketed text] with your specifics
  3. Set up as an automated sequence (triggered when someone joins your list)
  4. Send 1 email per day for 5 days, then 1 email per week ongoing

What to Send After the Welcome Sequence

  • Weekly: One helpful tip + one link to your content
  • Bi-weekly: New micro-tool announcement
  • Monthly: Roundup of best resources
  • Occasionally: Soft pitch for your digital product or affiliate recommendation

Bottom Line

Email is the only audience you truly own. Build your list from day one. Send value first, sell second. These templates are your starting point — customize them as you learn what your subscribers respond to.
