# Revenue Stacking — Multiple $0-Cost Income Streams

Don’t rely on one income source. Stack them. Here is how to combine affiliate marketing, digital products, and services using only free tools.

## The Revenue Stack

| Layer | Income Type | Effort to Start | Time to First $ |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Affiliate commissions | Low | 2–6 weeks |
| 2 | Digital product sales | Medium | 4–8 weeks |
| 3 | Service income | Medium | 1–3 weeks |
| 4 | Recurring subscriptions | High | 2–4 months |

## Layer 1: Affiliate Commissions (Start Today)

Promote products you already use. No product creation needed.

**Best instant-approval programs:**
- Systeme.io: 40% recurring commission
- ClickBank: Up to 90% on digital products
- ShareASale: Various merchants, 5–50%
- Gumroad: Varies by creator

**Where to place links:**
- Your micro-tool pages
- Medium and Dev.to articles
- Social media bios
- Email newsletters

**Expected earnings:** $0–$500/month in first 3 months

## Layer 2: Digital Product Sales (Launch by Week 4)

Create once, sell forever.

**Products you can build for free:**
- Notion templates (Notion free tier)
- Spreadsheet calculators (Google Sheets free)
- Checklists and SOPs (Google Docs → PDF)
- Micro-tools (HTML/JS, host on GitHub Pages)

**Storefronts:** Gumroad, Ko-fi (free to start, take % per sale)

**Expected earnings:** $0–$1,000/month in first 6 months

## Layer 3: Service Income (Fastest Cash)

Sell your time. Fastest path to first dollar.

**Services you can offer for free:**
- Freelance writing (Medium articles for clients)
- Social media management (organic posting)
- Notion setup (build workspaces for clients)
- Spreadsheet creation (custom calculators)
- Micro-tool setup (install calculators on client sites)

**Where to find clients:**
- LinkedIn organic outreach
- Reddit (r/forhire, r/slavelabour)
- Upwork free tier
- Your own website contact form

**Expected earnings:** $100–$2,000/month in first 3 months

## Layer 4: Recurring Subscriptions (Build Last)

The hardest but most valuable layer.

**Recurring models:**
- Paid newsletter ($5–$15/month)
- Notion template club (monthly new templates)
- Micro-tool premium features
- Community access (Discord, free to start)

**Expected earnings:** $0–$5,000+/month after 6+ months

## How to Stack Without Overwhelm

**Month 1:** Focus on Layer 1 (affiliate) + Layer 3 (services)
- Publish 3 articles/week with affiliate links
- Send 5 LinkedIn connection requests/day for services
- Goal: First $100 total

**Month 2:** Add Layer 2 (digital product)
- Launch one $7–$27 product on Gumroad
- Promote it in every article and social post
- Goal: First $500/month total

**Month 3–6:** Optimize all layers
- Double down on what’s working
- Add Layer 4 (recurring) if demand exists
- Goal: First $1,000+/month consistent

## The Math

| Layer | Price | Sales Needed for $1K/month |
|-------|-------|---------------------------|
| Affiliate (40% recurring on $27/mo) | $10.80/sale | 93 customers |
| Digital product | $27 | 37 sales |
| Service | $250/project | 4 projects |
| Recurring newsletter | $10/mo | 100 subscribers |

You don’t need all layers to hit $1K. Pick two and master them.

## Bottom Line

Revenue stacking reduces risk and increases income. Start with affiliate + services for fast cash. Add digital products for scale. Build recurring last. Stack them all and you have a real business.
