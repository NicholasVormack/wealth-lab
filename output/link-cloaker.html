Affiliate Link Cloaker

Create clean, branded redirect URLs for your affiliate links. Use your own domain instead of ugly affiliate URLs.

Your Link Destination Description
/go/systeme https://systeme.io/?sa=YOUR_ID All-in-one business platform — 40% recurring commission
/go/clickbank https://clickbank.com/ Digital products marketplace — instant approval
/go/gumroad https://gumroad.com/ Digital product storefront — free to start
/go/kofi https://ko-fi.com/ Donations + digital products — free tier
/go/amazon https://amazon.com/ Physical products affiliate — apply for account

How to Use

1. Replace the placeholder URLs in scripts/link-cloaker.mjs with your real affiliate links
2. Run node scripts/link-cloaker.mjs to regenerate redirect pages
3. Deploy the go/ folder to GitHub Pages alongside your micro-tools
4. Use yourdomain.com/go/systeme instead of long affiliate URLs
5. Each redirect page sends visitors to your affiliate link with a brief delay

Benefit: Clean links look more trustworthy, are easier to remember, and you can change the destination without updating all your content.
