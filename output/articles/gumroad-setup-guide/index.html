Gumroad Setup Guide — Sell Digital Products With $0

2026-11-14 · gumroad, digital-products, zero-dollar, storefront

Gumroad lets you sell digital products with no monthly fee. You only pay when you make a sale (10% + payment processing). Perfect for $0-capital builders.

Step 1: Create Your Account

  1. Go to gumroad.com and sign up for free
  2. Verify your email
  3. Complete your profile (add a photo and bio — buyers trust real people)
  4. Connect a payout method (PayPal or bank account)

Step 2: Create Your First Product

  1. Click "Add product" in your dashboard
  2. Choose "Digital product" (file) or "Membership" (recurring)
  3. Upload your file (PDF, ZIP, Notion link, etc.)
  4. Write a clear title and description
  5. Set your price (you can even set $0 for a lead magnet)
  6. Click "Publish"

Step 3: Get Your Product Link

After publishing, copy the product URL. It looks like: https://gumroad.com/l/your-product-name

This is the link you share everywhere.

Step 4: Add a Checkout Page (Optional but Recommended)

Gumroad lets you customize your checkout page:

  • Add your brand colors
  • Upload a logo
  • Write a compelling sales description
  • Add FAQs to reduce refund requests

Product Ideas That Sell

| Product | Format | Price Range | |---------|--------|-------------| | Notion template | Duplicate link | $7–$25 | | Spreadsheet calculator | Google Sheets link | $5–$15 | | Checklist/SOP | PDF | $7–$12 | | Short ebook | PDF | $9–$27 | | Email templates | Text/HTML files | $12–$27 | | Video course | MP4 or Vimeo link | $47–$97 |

Pricing Psychology

  • $7–$9: Impulse buy, no thinking required
  • $27: Standard digital product price point
  • $47–$97: Requires more trust, sell after free/cheap products
  • $0: Lead magnet to build your email list

Start with $0–$7 to get your first sales and reviews. Raise prices as you get testimonials.

Driving Traffic (All Free)

  • Micro-tools: Link to your Gumroad product from your calculator pages
  • Articles: Mention your product in relevant Medium/Dev.to posts
  • Social media: Link in bio on X, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn
  • Pinterest: Pin images of your product with link to Gumroad
  • Email: Promote to your newsletter list
  • Reddit: Share in relevant subreddits (read rules first)

Gumroad Features You Should Use

  • Affiliate program: Let others promote your product for a commission
  • Discount codes: Create limited-time offers
  • Upsells: Offer a related product after checkout
  • Subscriptions: Recurring revenue for memberships or software
  • Analytics: Track views, sales, and conversion rate

Bottom Line

Gumroad is free to start. Create one product, list it, and start sharing your link. Your first sale validates everything. Scale from there.
