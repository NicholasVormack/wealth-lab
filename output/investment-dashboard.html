Investment Setup Checklist

  • Open Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard account (free, $0 minimum)
  • Claim free stock bonuses (Robinhood, Webull)
  • Set up automatic monthly investment ($25–$100/month)
  • Buy index fund: FXAIX, FZROX, VOO, or VTI
  • Enable DRIP (dividend reinvestment)
  • Add dividend ETF: SCHD or VYM
  • Route side hustle income → emergency fund → investments
  • Update investment-data.json with real balances monthly
