Affiliate Marketing on Medium and Dev.to Without a Website

2026-11-13 · affiliate-marketing, medium, devto, zero-dollar

You do not need a website, email list, or paid ads to start affiliate marketing. Medium and Dev.to let you publish content with affiliate links for free, and several affiliate programs approve instantly.

Medium Strategy

Medium has built-in distribution. Articles can get picked up by Medium's algorithm and emailed to interested readers.

  1. Create a free Medium account
  2. Write articles that solve specific problems (e.g., "Best free tools for freelancers in 2026")
  3. Include affiliate links naturally in the content and your bio
  4. Publish 3-5 articles per week
  5. Repurpose the same articles to Dev.to

Article Topics That Convert

  • "7 free tools I use to run my side hustle" (affiliate links to each tool)
  • "How I made my first $100 online as a beginner" (include the platform you used)
  • "Best freelance rate calculator + how to price your services" (link to your calculator + relevant courses)
  • "Affiliate programs with instant approval for 2026" (link to the programs)

Dev.to Strategy

Dev.to is developer-focused but has strong business, productivity, and tooling sections.

  1. Create a free Dev.to account
  2. Write tutorials and tool reviews
  3. Include affiliate links to software, courses, and digital products
  4. Use tags like productivity, tools, webdev, sidehustle
  5. Cross-post to Medium (Dev.to supports cross-posting with canonical URLs)

Content Distribution Multiplier

Publish once, distribute everywhere:

  • Medium article → Dev.to cross-post → Reddit discussion → X thread → LinkedIn post
  • Each platform feeds traffic back to your affiliate links and your micro-tools

Rules to Follow

  1. Disclose affiliate relationships — Required by FTC and platform policies
  2. Don't spam — Provide genuine value first, links second
  3. Read subreddit rules — Some subreddits ban affiliate links entirely
  4. Focus on one niche — Don't promote unrelated products

Bottom Line

Medium + Dev.to + instant-approval affiliates = a zero-dollar affiliate machine. Publish helpful content daily, link to tools you actually use, and reinvest your first commissions into scaling.
