Gumroad takes 10% + $0.50 per sale on their free plan. There are cheaper alternatives. Here are the best free digital product storefronts for 2026.

## Platform Recommendations

### Gumroad
**Best for:** General digital products, easy setup, built-in audience discovery.
**Fee:** 10% + $0.50 per sale on free plan.
**Use when:** You want simplicity and don't mind higher fees for low volume.

### Payhip
**Best for:** Lowest fees at volume, product-focused storefront.
**Fee:** 5% free, drops to 2% on $29/mo Plus plan.
**Use when:** You're selling $500+/month and want to keep more margin.

### Ko-fi
**Best for:** Tips + digital products, audience-first creators.
**Fee:** 5% on sales, or 0% with Gold ($12/mo).
**Use when:** You have an engaged audience that tips, and sell occasional products.

### Lemon Squeezy
**Best for:** Digital products, global tax handling (they handle VAT/sales tax).
**Fee:** 5% + $0.50 per sale.
**Use when:** You sell internationally and want tax compliance handled.

### Beacons
**Best for:** Link-in-bio sellers who want shop + link page combined.
**Fee:** 9% free, 0% on Store Pro ($30/mo).
**Use when:** Your traffic comes from social media bios.
